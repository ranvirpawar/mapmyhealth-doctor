
# Map My Health Doctor Portal

A doctor-facing web portal for reviewing patient health data, managing appointments, and completing consultations connected to the Map My Health mobile app.

Live URL: https://ranvirpawar.github.io/mapmyhealth-doctor/

## Purpose

The Doctor Portal helps doctors view patient data collected from the mobile application and use it during consultation. Patients can book appointments from the mobile app, and those appointments become visible in the doctor portal for review and action.

The portal is designed as a clinical workspace where doctors can quickly understand a patient’s health status, connect for consultation, review trends, and create prescriptions.

## Key Features

- Doctor login and dashboard
- Appointment list for booked patient consultations
- Patient profile with basic demographic and clinical information
- Vitals overview such as BP, sugar, heart rate, SpO2, BMI, and risk score
- Patient trend charts for health history and progress tracking
- High-risk patient alerts and notifications
- Appointment details with symptoms, notes, priority, and consultation type
- Consultation workspace for video/in-clinic consultation flow
- Prescription creation workflow
- Lab order selection
- Past consultation history
- Reports view for patient test results
- Mobile responsive UI for use across desktop, tablet, and phone screens

## Product Flow

1. Patient uses the mobile app to maintain health details and book appointments.
2. Doctor sees the appointment in the Doctor Portal.
3. Doctor opens the appointment and reviews the patient profile.
4. Doctor checks vitals, trends, symptoms, history, and reports.
5. Doctor starts the consultation.
6. Doctor writes notes, adds diagnosis, creates prescription, and may order lab tests.
7. Consultation details and prescription become part of the patient record.

## Data Shown In Portal

- Patient demographics
- Current vitals
- Risk level and risk score
- Medical conditions
- Current medications
- Allergies
- Appointment history
- Consultation notes
- Lab reports
- Health trend charts
- Notifications and alerts
